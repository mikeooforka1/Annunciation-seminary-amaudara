Annunciation Seminary Amaudara, founded in 1984 by Late Bishop Anthony Gogo Nwedo CSSp., is a renowned Catholic seminary located in Amaudara, Aba, Abia State, Nigeria. The seminary is dedicated to the holistic formation of young men for the Catholic priesthood, emphasizing academic excellence, spiritual growth, leadership, and service. With modern facilities, a vibrant community, and a rich history, Annunciation Seminary Amaudara continues to produce outstanding priests and leaders who renew the face of the earth.
